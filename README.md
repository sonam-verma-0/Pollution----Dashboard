# Pollution----Dashboard

 Key Insights & Diagnostic Analysis

++ Overall AQI has improved from 2015 to 2020, with a sharp decline after 2018, indicating better air-quality outcomes.
++ Ahmedabad, Delhi, Patna, and Lucknow are the most polluted cities, while Aizawl, Shillong, and Ernakulam remain the cleanest.
++ Pollution hotspots are concentrated in North and Central India, especially the Indo-Gangetic belt.
++ Winter is the most polluted season, while monsoon shows the lowest AQI, confirming strong seasonal influence.
++ PM2.5 and PM10 are the dominant contributors to AQI across cities.
++ A strong positive correlation between PM2.5 and PM10 indicates common pollution sources such as traffic, construction, and dust.

Diagnostic Analysis 

++High AQI in northern cities is driven by dense population, traffic, industry, and regional factors.
++Winter pollution spikes occur due to temperature inversion and low wind movement.
++Lower AQI during monsoon results from rainfall washing out particulate matter.
++PM dominance indicates pollution is mainly from dust, construction, and vehicular emissions.

Recommendations to Control Pollution in Affected Cities

++Control PM2.5 & PM10 at source by tightening dust management at construction sites and enforcing mechanized road sweeping.
++Reduce vehicular emissions through stricter PUC checks, promotion of public transport, and EV adoption in high-AQI cities.
++Winter-specific action plans such as restrictions on biomass burning, firecracker control, and enhanced monitoring during peak months.
++Industrial emission control via continuous emission monitoring systems (CEMS) and penalties for non-compliance.
++City-level targeted policies focusing on hotspot zones rather than uniform national measures.
